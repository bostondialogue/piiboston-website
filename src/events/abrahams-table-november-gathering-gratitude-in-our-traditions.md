Join us for the next gathering of **Abraham’s Table: A Year of Dialogue, One Table, Many Faiths**, hosted by The Jack Satter House.

This month, we’ll come together to explore **“Gratitude in Our Traditions”** through reflections from different faith perspectives, meaningful conversation, and a shared meal.

### The Evening Will Include

- Reflections on gratitude in the different faith traditions
- Opportunity for dialogue between guests
- Light refreshments and a joint meal together
- Musical performance

### Invited Speakers

- **Fr. Xavier Arulraj** — Saint Anthony of Padua Parish
- **Jurgen Shehu** — Brandeis University
- **Rabbi Lior Nevo** — The Jack Satter House

Come to listen, connect, share, and celebrate the spirit of gratitude across our traditions. All are welcome!
