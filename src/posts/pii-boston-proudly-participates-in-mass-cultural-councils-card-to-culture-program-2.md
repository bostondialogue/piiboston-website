### We are proud to participate in [Mass Cultural Council’s Card to Culture program](https://massculturalcouncil.org/organizations/card-to-culture/) in collaboration with the Department of Transitional Assistance, the Department of Public Health’s WIC Nutrition Program, the Massachusetts Health Connector, and hundreds of organizations by making cultural programming accessible to those for whom cost is a participation barrier.

All Peace Islands Institute programs and events are free for families of EBT card holders. They also receive a 50% discount on everything on sale (meals, gifts, souvenirs, etc.)

See the complete list of participating organizations offering [EBT](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/ebt-card-to-culture-organizations), [WIC](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/get-discounts-with-your-wic-card#cultural-organizations-), and [ConnectorCare](https://www.mahealthconnector.org/learn/plan-information/connectorcare-plans/connectorcare-card-to-culture) discounts.
