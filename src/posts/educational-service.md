The human factor lies at the base of all our problems, for all problems begin and end with people. Education is the best vehicle for a defect-free (or almost free) well-functioning social system or for a good life beyond the grave. In this respect, we strongly believe that just as teaching is the most sacred profession, the best service to one’s country or nation is education.

Education that leaves no one behind, inclusive with peace and harmony.
