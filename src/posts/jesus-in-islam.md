In the spirit of interfaith understanding and harmony, today we delve into an often lesser-known aspect of Islamic belief – the profound respect and reverence for Jesus (Isa in Arabic). In Islam, Jesus is regarded as one of the most significant and beloved prophets, his life and teachings holding great importance.
