Peace Islands Instıtute Boston collaborates with volunteers to deliver humanitarian aid and disaster relief to vulnerable communities both domestically and internationally.

Our teams offer relief in the form of financial, emotional and physical support, in order to meet the needs of people ravaged by disaster and other misfortune. The foundation collects and distributes supplies to families, individuals and institutions.
