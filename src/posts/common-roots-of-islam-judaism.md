A special interfaith lecture and discussion exploring the shared history, traditions, and deep connections between Islam and Judaism. In a time of rising antisemitism and Islamophobia, understanding our shared heritage is more critical than ever.

The evening featured Rabbi Leonard Gordon, Chair of the National Council of Synagogues, who facilitates interfaith dialogue for Reform, Reconstructionist, and Conservative Jewish communities and frequently teaches Introduction to Islam to Jewish audiences.

**Event Details**

- Date & Time: Monday, August 31, 2026 at 7:00 PM
- Location: Temple Israel, 125 Pond Street, Sharon, MA 02067
- Hosts: New England Region of FJMC (NERFJMC) & Peace Islands Institute
- Admission: Free for Peace Islands members; refreshments served
