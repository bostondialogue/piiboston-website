A heartfelt thank you to the Jack Satter House for graciously hosting such a meaningful Interfaith Thanksgiving Service. Your hospitality and warm spirit made the morning truly special.​

Our deep gratitude also goes to all the speakers and community leaders who enriched the program with their presence and reflections—Mayor Patrick Keefe, City Councilor Ira Novoselsky, State Representative Jeffrey Rosario Turco, Esq., Fr. Xavier Arulraj, Pastor Tim Bogertman, Rabbi Lior Nevo, and all who shared prayers, music, and messages of peace.​

It was an honor to join this gathering as the Executive Director of Peace Islands Institute Boston and to stand alongside friends from many traditions. Moments like this remind us how powerful unity, gratitude, and compassion can be
