### May 23–31, 2026 · $2,000 + airline tickets

A 9-day journey to Tanzania combining safari adventures with the beauty of the Zanzibar Islands and cultural exchange opportunities.

**Highlights included:**

- A visit to Mikumi National Park and a safari
- Experiencing the culture and history of a Maasai village
- A Bagamoyo city tour and other sightseeing
- Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site
- Prison Island, home to century-old tortoises and swimming spots
- The Blue Lagoon and a Spice Farm visit
- Humanitarian work, including orphanage and school visits, water well openings, and hunger relief activities

**Included:** all meals, park and museum fees, a professional driver and guide, in-country transportation, and accommodation.
**Not included:** international airfare, personal shopping, travel insurance/visa fees, and vaccinations.

A $500 deposit was required to secure a spot, with the remaining balance due one month before the trip.
