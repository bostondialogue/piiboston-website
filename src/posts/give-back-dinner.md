Collaboration. BDF and its sister organizations will team up with the Revere Warming Center & Malden Warming Center to provide dinners during the month of Ramadan for those experiencing homelessness. For more info, contact info at piima.org

Voluntarism. Become a volunteer at the Revere Warming Center ( Adrienne, amaguire at revere.org ) and Malden Warming Center (www_maldenwarmingcenter.org/volunteer). There are many opportunities for individuals and groups to impact the important work of caring for individuals experiencing homelessness in Revere and Malden cities. Support. The Warming Centers will rely on the philanthropy of the community to continue this life-changing center. We have an obligation towards humanity, and the least we can do is offer them something to eat, drink, clothing, and volunteer at warming stations during the harsh wintertime. To make a donation, kindly scan the QR code provided in the flyer.

***There is currently a community effort to establish and provide a warming station in Revere. The aim is to provide a warm and secure warming station for those who are homeless during the winter months of 2025 and beyond, recognizing each person’s inherent dignity and unique circumstances.
