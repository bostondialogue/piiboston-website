A relaxing and creative Calligraphy Workshop with calligrapher and lettering artist Benjamin Aysan. Whether a complete beginner or looking to explore the art of beautiful writing, this hands-on workshop was a wonderful opportunity to learn, create, and take home a finished piece.

**What's included:**

- All calligraphy materials provided
- Calligraphy workbook & pen to keep
- Beginner-friendly, step-by-step instruction
- Create your own artwork on cardstock, canvas, or a ceramic plate

**Duration:** approximately 2 hours
**Price:** $69 per person
**Location:** Azerbaijan Cultural Center, Allston, MA
