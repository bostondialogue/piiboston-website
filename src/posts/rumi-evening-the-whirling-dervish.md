> "Love is the bridge between you and everything." – Rumi

A spiritual and musical evening inspired by the life and poetry of Jalaluddin Rumi, featuring the graceful whirling dervish (Sema) ceremony, reflections on Rumi's message of love and unity, and uplifting music.

**Date:** Thursday, December 19, 2025, 6:00–8:00 PM
**Venue:** Central Congregational Church, UCC – Fellowship Hall, One Worthen Street, Chelmsford, MA 01824

**Program included:**

- Whirling dervish (Sema) performance
- Spiritual readings from Rumi
- Musical presentations
- A short talk on Rumi's message of love and compassion
- Tea and light refreshments

All were welcome — people of all faiths and backgrounds.
