A special Community Interfaith Iftar Dinner — a meaningful evening of reflection, connection, and a shared meal breaking fast together during the month of Ramadan.

**Event Details**

- Date: Saturday, March 7, 2026
- Time: 5:00–7:00 PM
- Location: North Church, 896 Main St., Woburn, MA 01801

Hosted by Peace Islands Institute Boston, Boston Dialogue Foundation, North Church Woburn (United Church of Christ), and Know Your Neighbor Woburn. Neighbors of all faiths and backgrounds came together to share a meal, hear reflections from faith and community leaders, and engage in conversations that foster mutual understanding, respect, and lasting community. The dinner was free and open to all.
