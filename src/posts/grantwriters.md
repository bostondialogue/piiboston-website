The appointed grant writer will be responsible for researching and identifying funding opportunities for the organization. They will also be responsible for drafting polished grant proposals to secure funding for the organization. The grant writer’s duties will include researching deadlines, drafting grant requests and submitting reports for approval. Our organization will provide remote training, software subscriptions, and other necessary resources to assist the grant writer in their task. Bonus commission of 10% will be awarded to grant writers upon successful grant approval and completion of all requirements. Interns & Community service volunteers.
