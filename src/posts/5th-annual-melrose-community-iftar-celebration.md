### Saturday, April 8th, 2023 @ 6:00-9:00 pm at Memorial Hall (590 Main Street, Melrose)

You are cordially invited to join the Melrose Human Rights Commission for the 5th Annual Melrose Community Iftar Celebration. All are welcome!

The program will include guest speakers, musical performances, and activities for children.

Program details:

- 6:00 p.m. – Doors Open

- 6:15 – 7:15 p.m. – Musical Performances, Activities for children & Speaking Program

- 7:17 p.m. – Athan (“call to prayer”) at sunset

- 7:20 p.m. – Iftar (“breaking the fast”) Dinner

- 8:30 p.m. – The event concludes

- Suggested Donation at the door: $20 per family

Presented in partnership with Malden Islamic Center and Melrose Human Rights Commission
