### Become an Intern

### Explore Internship Opportunities at Peace Islands Institute

Peace Islands Institute Boston invites you to join our internship program in the Greater Boston area. As an intern, you’ll play a pivotal role in fostering a peaceful society, emphasizing the strength of diversity through education and relationship-building efforts. Our program offers a unique chance to engage with diverse communities, contribute to impactful projects, and develop skills essential for your future career. Embrace this opportunity to be part of a team dedicated to creating a more inclusive and understanding world.

Apply for an internship with Peace Islands Institute Boston today and step into a world of opportunity and growth.
